Hotel Lake Advisory Association

Biomonitoring Programs: Yes. Their related group, the Hotel Lake Stewards, records activities of fish, birds, aquatic invertebrates and mammals on the lake, and completes extensive water quality monitoring in conjunction with the Ministry of Environment and UBC. See a story about their work on jellyfish on the Current Research page.

Education Programs: No. However, their website is in large part devoted to education about Hotel Lake and related topics.

Biomonitoring and Community Science Volunteer Opportunities: No current opportunities.

Website: https://www.hotellakeadvisory.com
Location: Garden Bay
Contact: hotellakesave@gmail.com
