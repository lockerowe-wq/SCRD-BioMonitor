Sunshine Coast Friends of Forage Fish

Biomonitoring Methods

Forage Fish: At sampling sites 4L of beach sand is collected along a 30m transect. Content is sifted and vortexed, then examined in the lab for eggs. These sites are sampled regularly for most months of the year.
